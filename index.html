Key Exemptions & Rebates

Social Security Fund (SSF)

If you contribute to the SSF, the 1% Social Security Tax is generally waived.

Female Tax Rebate

A 10% tax credit is provided on total tax liability for female taxpayers whose only income is remuneration.

Medical Tax Credit

Up to NPR 750 or actual approved medical expenses (whichever is lower) can be claimed as a rebate.
